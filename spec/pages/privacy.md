# Privacy policy

<!-- legal review required before publication; last updated: launch date -->

## Who is responsible

Mobiera SAS, NIT 900662462-4, Cra 13A 86A-42, Bogotá D.C., Colombia, is the
data controller for mobiera.io. Data-protection contact: privacy@mobiera.com.

## What we collect

**Contact form.** Name, email, organization, topic and message, and a CV if
you apply for a role. We use them to answer you and, for applications, to
assess your profile. Submissions are stored for 24 months, applications for 12
months unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

**Analytics.** With your consent, anonymized usage statistics (pages visited,
device type, approximate region) to improve the site. No analytics cookie is
set before you accept. See the cookie policy.

**Server logs.** IP address, user agent and requested URL, kept for 30 days for
security and troubleshooting.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

## Legal basis

Your consent for analytics; our legitimate interest in answering your message
and keeping the site secure; the steps needed before a contract when you ask
about our products.

## Processors

The site runs on infrastructure operated by Mobiera. Form submissions are
routed to our internal tools; analytics, if you consent, to our analytics
provider. <!-- name the providers -->

## Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, under
Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and, where it applies, the GDPR. Write to
privacy@mobiera.com. We answer within 15 business days.

## Changes

We publish changes here with the date of the update.
