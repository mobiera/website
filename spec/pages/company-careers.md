# Code that runs in a live network.

We build platforms that mobile operators depend on every day, and the
verifiable credential systems that will let people, services and AI agents
prove who they are. Small team, real production systems, standards implemented
to the letter.

## Why work here

**Real systems.** What you ship runs on SMSCs and SIM cards at 25+ operators,
or on the Verana network. Bugs are visible; so is good work.

**Two fields at once.** Telecom protocols from the 1990s and decentralized
identity standards from this decade, in the same codebase and the same week.

**Distributed, bilingual.** Colombia, Peru, Bolivia, Panama, Spain and Algeria.
Spanish and English every day. Written communication first.
