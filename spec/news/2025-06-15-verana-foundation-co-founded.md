Mobiera is a founding member of the Verana Foundation, with 2060 OÜ and
Orchestrating Identity.

The Foundation is the non-profit steward of the open trust layer: it owns the
Verifiable Trust and Verifiable Public Registry specifications, stewards the
open-source software under Apache 2.0, and grows the ecosystem around it. It is
in formation, stewarded by 2060 OÜ. The live network is governed and secured by
a separate body, the Verana Council.

Mobiera contributes to the Foundation's Specification working group and its
Reference Implementation working group, and brings telecom channels, SMS, SIM
toolkit and phone-number verification, into the trust layer.

Read more: [Mobiera and Verana](/trust/verana) · [veranafoundation.org](https://veranafoundation.org)
