# Sitemap and navigation

## Header

Logo (normal variant; reduced mark under 640 px) · Verifiable Credentials ·
Certification · Telecom · Company · News · [Contact]

No login. Documentation links live on product pages and in the footer.

An optional one-line announcement bar may carry one dated item at a time (a
launch, an event) and is removed when the item is past.

## URL tree

```text
/                        Home
/telecom                 Telecom software and services
/telecom/aircast         Aircast: STK push marketing, messaging, OTA campaigns
/telecom/ai-agents       AI One: AI agents for customer support
/trust                   Verifiable credentials and trust networks
/trust/services          Ecosystem design, issuer and verifier services, wallets and agents
/trust/verana            Mobiera and Verana
/certification           Verana certification: training and exams for integrators in Latin America
/company                 About Mobiera
/company/careers         Careers
/news                    News and articles
/news/<slug>             One page per item
/contact                 Contact form
/privacy                 Privacy policy
/cookies                 Cookie policy
```

Fourteen pages plus one per news item.
