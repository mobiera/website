The Aircast documentation portal is live at docs.mobiera.io.

It covers the platform end to end: concepts and architecture, the campaign
engine, scalability and throughput shaping; the developer references for the
Sleepy Push, USTK Push and messaging APIs, file drop and webhooks; the Sleepy
and Micro applets with their installation guides; the operator manual for
campaigns, files, TPS rules, lists and statistics; and the infrastructure
section for prerequisites, deployment topologies, high availability, security,
monitoring, backups, scaling, upgrade and rollback.

The portal replaces the document sets previously delivered per project. It is
English-only and updated with each release.

Read more: [docs.mobiera.io](https://docs.mobiera.io) · [Aircast](/telecom/aircast)
