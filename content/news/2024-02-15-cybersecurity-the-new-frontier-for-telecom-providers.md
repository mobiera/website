Digital security threats keep escalating, and the need for protection with
them. That is an opportunity for telecom providers to extend their services
beyond connectivity: cybersecurity for consumers and for businesses, as a
service, with a new revenue stream attached.

## The growing need

Cyberattacks are more frequent and more sophisticated, and connected devices,
faster networks and remote work widen the exposure. Telecom providers sit at
the center of internet access, which puts them in a position to act.

**Consumers** are more connected than ever and rarely have the expertise to
protect themselves. A provider can offer protection that is easy to activate
and needs no configuration.

**Businesses** face greater risks, from data breaches to ransomware. A
provider can offer secure communication channels, data protection and network
security sized to the business.

## A new revenue stream

Traditional revenue from voice and SMS is shrinking. Cybersecurity as a
value-added service answers three needs at once.

- **Retention.** A complete offer reduces churn.
- **Differentiation.** Security is a selling point in a crowded market.
- **Partnerships.** Working with established security vendors shortens time to
  market and limits the investment.

## What it takes

- Expertise, developed or brought in through partners.
- An understanding of what consumers and businesses each need.
- Compliance with data-protection rules, and the trust that follows from it.
- Education: customers adopt what they understand.

## Conclusion

Cybersecurity is a new frontier for telecom providers: a market need, a growth
avenue, and a safer digital environment for their customers.
